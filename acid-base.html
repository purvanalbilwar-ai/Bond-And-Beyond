Meet acids and bases

Acids and bases are important groups of substances. Their reactions help explain everything from laboratory chemistry to processes in living things.

What is an acid?

In water, an acid increases the amount of hydronium ions (H₃O⁺). In the Brønsted–Lowry definition, an acid donates a proton (H⁺) to another substance.

What is a base?

A base can accept a proton. In water, some bases increase the amount of hydroxide ions (OH⁻). The Lewis definition describes a base as an electron-pair donor.

pH 0 — 7 — 14

For dilute water-based solutions at about 25°C, pH below 7 is acidic, pH 7 is neutral, and pH above 7 is basic. The exact pH depends on the solution and its concentration.

Why are acids and bases important?

They have useful roles in nature, products, and chemical processes.

🫀

Living systems

Stomach acid helps digestion, while the body carefully controls blood pH so cells can work properly.

🧪

Making materials

Acids and bases are used in making fertilisers, medicines, soaps, batteries, and many other materials.

⚖️

Neutralisation

An acid and a base can react to form water and a salt. This reaction is useful in laboratories and some treatment processes.

🌱

Soil and water

Measuring and adjusting pH can help manage soil for plants and control conditions in water treatment.

🍋

Food and flavour

Natural acids give foods a sour taste and are used in food preparation and preservation.

🔬

Understanding reactions

Acid–base ideas help explain how substances transfer protons, form ions, and react with each other.
